Design file requirements — read before sending

  • Format: PNG, JPG, BMP, or GIF. WEBP files are not accepted.
  • One image, two parts: a PRINT part (the artwork/pattern to print) and a CUT part (the outline to cut), both in the same file.
  • The CUT part must be a solid shape on a transparent background — clean edges, no jagged lines, saved as PNG with alpha (not a white background).
  • Keep the real-world size: if using Photoshop/Illustrator, export at 300 DPI so the physical size (mm) matches the image.
  • Using Google Drive? Share a link to each individual image file (not a folder), with sharing set to "Anyone with the link".

Wood

CNC-cut to match your design exactly, in a range of thicknesses depending on your piece.

Acrylic

Clear, frosted, or colored — cut precisely along your design's outline.

Each order should use a single material and thickness, since pieces are processed in batches from the same sheet of material. If you need different materials or thicknesses, please place separate orders.

I don't have Google Drive — can I send the image another way?

Yes — send the design directly by email or message, no Drive required.

My image doesn't have a transparent background — will you still accept it?

We can still take it, but it may take longer since it needs adjusting — it's best to separate the CUT part onto a transparent background (PNG alpha) before sending for faster, more accurate processing.

What if I need several materials or thicknesses in one order?

Please split them into separate orders by material/thickness — pieces are processed in batches from the same sheet of material.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

It depends on material, thickness, and quantity — send your design by email or message for a quote and a specific timeline.
